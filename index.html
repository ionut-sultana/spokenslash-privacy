SpokenSlash Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · updated 16 October 2026

SpokenSlash is a personal publishing tool. Its only job is to post the SpokenSlash channel's own short videos (narrated stories) to the SpokenSlash pages it is connected to: the SpokenSlash Facebook Page, and the channel's YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is run by the channel owner for that channel alone and has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • On Facebook, the access the Page admin grants to the SpokenSlash Page (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts), used only to upload Reels to that Page and to check that each upload finished processing.
  • On TikTok, the owner's own account through Login Kit and the Content Posting API (user.info.basic, video.publish). The app reads the account's ID, display name and the posting options TikTok allows it (such as which visibility levels are available), uploads the channel's videos, and checks that each one finished processing.
  • On YouTube, the owner's own channel, used to upload the channel's videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read the channel's own view statistics.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data about people who view, follow or comment on the channel's videos on any platform.
  • It does not read messages, comments, followers or anyone else's profile.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data, and it shows no ads.

Where data is kept

The access tokens that let the app post are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared. The app keeps a local record of which videos were posted where (video IDs and links), so nothing is posted twice. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform a video is being posted to.

Deleting data and removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, then remove SpokenSlash.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app's Settings and privacy, remove SpokenSlash from the apps connected to the account.
  • YouTube: in your Google Account, open Third-party connections and remove SpokenSlash.

Removing access stops the app from posting and invalidates its stored tokens. To ask for any record about you to be deleted, email the address below; requests are handled within 30 days. Because the app does not collect data about other people, there is normally nothing to delete.
