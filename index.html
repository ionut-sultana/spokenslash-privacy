SpokenSlash Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

SpokenSlash is a personal publishing tool. Its only job is to post the SpokenSlash channel's own short videos (narrated stories) to the SpokenSlash pages it is connected to: the SpokenSlash Facebook Page, and the channel's YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is run by the channel owner for that channel alone and has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • On Facebook, the access the Page admin grants to the SpokenSlash Page (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts), used only to upload Reels to that Page and to check that each upload finished processing.
  • On YouTube and TikTok, the owner's own account, used only to upload the channel's videos.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data about people who view, follow or comment on the Page.
  • It does not read messages, comments or anyone else's profile.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data, and it shows no ads.

Where data is kept

The access tokens that let the app post are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared. The app keeps a local record of which videos were posted where (video IDs and links), so nothing is posted twice.

Deleting data and removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, then remove SpokenSlash. To ask for any record about you to be deleted, email the address below; requests are handled within 30 days. Because the app does not collect data about other people, there is normally nothing to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
